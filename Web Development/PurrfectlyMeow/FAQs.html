How does mealtime work? ▼

We follow a strict feeding schedule to maintain routine and portion control. Cats thrive on consistency, and our scheduled mealtimes provide that.We understand that cats appreciate the occasional treat. With your permission, we can include special treats in their diet to make mealtime even more enjoyable.

I have an older cat. What actions will you take to guarantee their comfort? ▼

We schedule regular veterinary check-ups to monitor your cat's health and address any age-related issues promptly. Older cats often have different dietary needs. We provide a tailored diet plan that considers their age, weight, and any specific health conditions.

What actions will you take to safeguard the health of my cat? ▼

We maintain open communication with pet owners. If we notice any changes in your cat's health or behavior, we'll promptly inform you and work together to address any concerns. We keep detailed records of your cat's health and activities during their stay, which you can access at any time.

BOARDING

Back to Top

Can I visit my pet during their stay, and how can I stay updated on their activities and health? ▼

At our boarding facility, we understand how important it is for pet owners to stay connected with their furry friends while they're in our care. You are absolutely welcome to visit your pet during their stay. We encourage pet owners to spend quality time with their pets to help them feel comfortable and loved.

Additionally, we offer various methods for you to stay updated on your pet's activities and health. Our staff keeps detailed records of your pet's daily routines, including feeding, exercise, and any special requests you may have. You can access these records at any time for peace of mind.

Furthermore, we provide photo and video updates through our online portal or mobile app, allowing you to see how your pet is doing and what fun activities they're engaged in. We believe that keeping you informed and connected with your pet enhances their boarding experience and ensures your peace of mind. If you ever have specific questions or concerns, our team is readily available to assist you and provide updates as needed. Your pet's well-being and your satisfaction are our top priorities!

What are the accommodation options for my pet during their stay at your boarding facility? ▼

Standard Kennels: Our standard kennels are spacious and comfortable, providing a secure and cozy environment for your pet. They come with soft bedding and regular meals.

Luxury Suites: For pets who enjoy a more luxurious experience, we offer spacious suites with comfortable beds and plenty of room to stretch out. These suites often include large windows for natural light.

Private Cottages: If your pet prefers privacy, our private cottages provide a secluded and quiet space. They come with premium bedding, individual play areas, and additional amenities.

Group Play Areas: In addition to their accommodations, all pets have access to group play areas where they can socialize, play, and exercise under supervision.

We believe in providing a comfortable and enjoyable stay for your pet, and our range of accommodation options allows you to choose the one that best suits their preferences and needs. Your pet's happiness and well-being are our top priorities, and we take every step to ensure they have a wonderful stay with us.

TRAINING

What types of training programs do you offer for cats?▼

At Purrfectly Meow, we offer a range of specialized training programs designed to enhance your cat's behavior, socialization, and overall well-being. Our cat training programs include:

Obedience Training: Our obedience training programs are tailored to teach your cat basic commands and behaviors, such as litter box training, recall, and leash walking. This helps ensure a harmonious relationship between you and your feline companion.

Behavioral Training: If your cat exhibits specific behavioral issues, our experienced trainers can work with you to address them. Whether it's excessive scratching, aggression, or anxiety, we have training solutions to promote positive behavior.

Socialization Training: Socialization is crucial for kittens and newly adopted cats. We provide socialization training to help your cat adapt to new environments, people, and other pets. This training is especially beneficial for reducing stress and anxiety.

Enrichment Programs: Cats thrive on mental and physical stimulation. Our enrichment programs include puzzle toys, interactive games, and activities that engage your cat's natural instincts, such as hunting and exploring.

How experienced are your trainers?▼

Our trainers are highly experienced and dedicated professionals with a deep passion for working with animals. Each member of our training team has years of hands-on experience in pet behavior and training. They continuously stay updated with the latest training techniques and animal behavior research to provide the best possible care and guidance for your beloved pets. Rest assured, your pet will be in expert hands, and our trainers are committed to helping them thrive and reach their full potential.
