Welcome to Purrfectly Meow Cat Service Organization, your trusted partner for feline welfare. We are a dedicated team of cat lovers who share a deep love for our bearded companions.

About Us

Since 2020, we have been providing fun and dependable cat care services parents and kitties love. We're more than just a group of cat enthusiasts; we're a dedicated team of passionate individuals committed to enhancing the lives of our feline friends and their humans. Each member of our team brings a unique set of skills and a deep understanding of feline behavior to ensure the best possible care for your cats.
We provide the highest level of care, support and service to cats and their devoted owners. We know cats are more than just pets; they are beloved family members and they deserve the best.

Our History

Founded in 2018 by a group of cat lovers who wanted to create a safe, nurturing environment for cats in need, Purrfectly Meow has grown into a respected and trusted cat service organization. Over the years, we've expanded our services, honed our expertise, and built a community of cat enthusiasts who share our passion for these wonderful creatures.

Inception

2019

PurrfectlyMeow.com is born out of a passion for feline companions. It starts as a simple blog where cat owners share their stories and tips on cat care. The website's founder, Foo Kar Yeng, adopts a rescue cat named Olivia, inspiring her to create a community for cat lovers. we conceived the idea for a comprehensive cat service management website, followed by market research in March to validate the concept. Also, we assembled a team of web developers, cat care experts, and designers, and by July, we had planned out the website's structure, features, and design.

Cat Services Programs

2019-2020

In January, we began developing various cat services programs, including cat daycare, grooming, boarding, and training. In June, these services were integrated into the website, providing users with a wide range of cat-related offerings. Throughout September, rigorous testing and refinements were made to ensure the services worked seamlessly.

User Registration, Booking, and Donation System

2020-2021

In April, user registration functionality was introduced, allowing cat owners and enthusiasts to create accounts. By July, an integrated booking system was added, enabling users to schedule cat services, and in September, a donation system was introduced to support cat welfare causes.

User Feedback and Emergency Contact Features

2021-2022

A feedback mechanism was implemented to collect user suggestions and enhance the website's features. By May, an emergency contact feature was added, allowing cat owners to provide crucial information. In October, plans were initiated to make the website mobile-responsive, responding to user demand and industry trends.

Mobile-Responsive Design

2023

In March, the website underwent a redesign to become fully mobile-responsive, ensuring a seamless user experience on various devices. By June, the mobile-responsive design underwent thorough testing and optimization, leading to an official launch in October, offering users a user-friendly experience on both desktop and mobile platforms.
