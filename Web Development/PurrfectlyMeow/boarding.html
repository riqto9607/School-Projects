OVERNIGHT CAT BOARDING SERVICES

Enjoy Peace of Mind While You’re on the Go

Headed out of town but can’t take your pup with you? Though it would be wonderful to take our dogs with us, it’s not always feasible. Camp Bow Wow® offers safe, fun, and trustworthy overnight dog boarding services. With us, your dog will enjoy a full day of play in our spacious play yards. We’ll make sure your dog is having the time of their life throughout their stay!

SAFETY STANDARDS:
Certified Meow Counselors® who are extensively trained in cat behavior, pet CPR, and pet first aid. Assurance that all our Meowers have passed the Meower Interview, are spayed or neutered, and are current on vaccinations. Live web cams that allow you to check in on your kitty on your computer or mobile device
A MEOW ABOVE THE REST
Your cat is your family – at Purrfectly Meow, we understand that. As cat lovers ourselves, we take your kitty’s care very seriously. That’s why we think of ourselves as so much more than just a cat boarding service. We are here to provide love and support for your furry family member while you are away—filling that gap as best we can until your return.
ALL INCLUSIVE PRICING:
Get more play in your stay at MEOW! We offer premier care with no hidden fees. Whether your dog is here for the day or overnight, they'll play all day with furry friends and get lots of TLC from our Meow Counselors. With Purrfectly Meow, you get the best care at the best value once you make a reservation.

Day Care included in overnight stays. All-day play in indoor and outdoor play yards. Individual cozy Cabin and nightly Campfire Treat for Overnight Meowers. No charge for medication administration and other special requests. Lots of individual attention and TLC from Meow Counselors.
