Giving Back

Lending a Helping Pur

Purrfectly Meow® has always been and continues to be committed to giving back to animals in need. The Purrfectly Meow Foundation® is a non-profit organization through which we promote the happiness and health of our furry family members in need.

More about the Purrfectly Meow Foundation

Countless cats are denied urgent, necessary medical care every year due to the high cost of veterinary treatment. The Purrfectly Meow Foundation is a lifeline for many struggling pet parents who are facing an impossible choice. By easing the financial burdens of treating a sick or injured cats, the Purrfectly Meow Foundation helps to prevent the heartbreaking loss of furry family members in need of medical attention.

Cat's Lives We've Changed

Olivia's Journey to Healing

Meet Olivia, a 2-year-old mackerel tabby cat who had her playful spirit hampered by a serious injury. Her owner, Sarah, faced a financial challenge. Fortunately, the Purrfectly Meow® Foundation provided financial support for Olivia's surgery and rehabilitation. Now, Olivia is recovering well, bringing joy back to Sarah's life.

Double the Love: The Duo of Cats

In their cozy home, Marchael, a serene calico cat, and Jordan, a spirited cyprus cat, received vital help from the Purrfectly Meow® Foundation when Marchael fell ill, relieving their owner of unexpected medical expenses.

Lily's Journey to Health

Lily, a 3-year-old Persian cat known for her grace and beauty, faced a sudden illness that left her weak. Emily, her owner, sought financial help to restore her health. Kind Hearts for Purrfectly Meow® Foundation stepped in, providing the necessary medical treatment. Lily is now back to her majestic self, bringing joy to Emily's life.
